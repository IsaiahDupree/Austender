Description

This tender is for the construction of new government facilities in Sydney's CBD. The project involves:

  • Construction of a 6-story office building
  • Underground parking facilities
  • Integration of sustainable design features
  • Landscaping and external works

The project aims to provide modern, accessible facilities for government services while meeting high environmental standards.
